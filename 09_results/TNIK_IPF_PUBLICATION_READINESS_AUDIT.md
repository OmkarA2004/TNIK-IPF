## 6. Important scientific limitations

- No experimental TNIK validation is available.
- Docking scores do not establish experimental binding affinity or inhibition.
- The Ponatinib cell-lysate Kinobeads Kd observation is not a purified-TNIK IC50 result.
- The current short restrained MD simulations do not establish long-term unrestrained stability.
- Computational prioritisation does not establish IPF therapeutic efficacy.

## 7. Recommended manuscript framing

Present this work as an exploratory computational drug-repurposing and candidate-prioritisation study targeting TNIK in the context of IPF.

Do not describe Ponatinib or Tepotinib as experimentally confirmed TNIK inhibitors.
